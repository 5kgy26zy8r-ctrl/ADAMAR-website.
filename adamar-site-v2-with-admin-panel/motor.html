How it works

Every Adamar is built around a high-performance brushless motor, chosen for the instant, effortless torque and seamless acceleration it delivers the moment you open the throttle. It's paired with an aerodynamic frame, sophisticated safety systems, and smart connectivity that work together to keep the ride smooth from the first metre to the last.

Because the motor platform is shared across the range, each model simply tunes it differently — for low-speed torque on the Hunter, sustained cruising power on the Highway, or outright performance on the Max.

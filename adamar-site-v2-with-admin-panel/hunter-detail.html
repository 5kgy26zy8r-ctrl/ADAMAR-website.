Advanced motor

High-performance electric motor delivering instant torque and smooth acceleration. Compact, efficient, and brushless for low maintenance and maximum efficiency — durable and lightweight, built for high torque and long-range performance.

Battery and BMS

A lithium battery is a lightweight, efficient rechargeable energy source built for electric vehicles. The Battery Management System (BMS) monitors voltage, current, and temperature for safe operation, preventing overcharging and thermal issues, which enhances performance and lifespan.

High-tech controller

A high-tech controller manages complex systems with cutting-edge algorithms and real-time monitoring, ensuring optimal performance, safety, and flexibility — enhancing reliability and efficiency with fast processing and improved connectivity.

Advanced Tec charger

The Advanced Tec Charger provides efficient charging in a compact design, with safety mechanisms to prevent overheating and durability for long-lasting use — an energy-efficient design backed by a comprehensive warranty.

Our story

We think different

Welcome to Adamar Electric Motorcycles, where cutting-edge innovation seamlessly intertwines with a commitment to sustainability. Our dedication to quality is evident in our meticulous manufacturing processes and the handcrafted assembly of each motorcycle. Every bike we produce is a testament to our unwavering focus on performance and environmental responsibility.

Our strategic partnerships with businesses around the globe enable us to broaden our horizons while remaining steadfast in our core principles. These collaborations enhance our ability to deliver exceptional electric motorcycles that resonate with riders who value both style and substance. We embrace the philosophy that simplicity can lead to sophistication, merging modern design with practical functionality.

We invite you to embark on an exhilarating journey with us as we pave the way toward a more sustainable future. Our electric motorcycles are not just vehicles; they represent a lifestyle choice that prioritizes eco-friendliness without compromising on performance or aesthetics. Join us in redefining the riding experience, one ride at a time.

How we got here

The Adamar timeline

Concept

Adamar International begins as a design studio, working on vehicle concepts across several categories.

Platform

Development starts on a single shared electric drivetrain platform, built to be tuned for very different kinds of riding.

EGO Urban

The first model reveal — a compact, city-focused electric moped, shown in full CGI detail before physical prototypes.

The range

Hunter, Highway, Zero, Urban, Max, Classic, and Surf take shape as seven distinct expressions of the same platform.

Today

Adamar continues building and refining every model in New Zealand, for New Zealand roads first.

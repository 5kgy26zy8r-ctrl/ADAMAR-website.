Highway, up close

The Highway is a statement of freedom, defined by its commanding cruiser aesthetic and built entirely for the open road. With the option to carry two additional batteries on board, range anxiety becomes a thing of the past. Highway is your invitation to chase the never-ending horizon.

Advanced motor

Groundbreaking materials and brilliant engineering unleash astonishing power while dramatically cutting energy consumption — essential for minimising environmental impact and driving the machinery of the future forward.

Battery and BMS

A lithium battery with exceptional energy storage, paired with a Battery Management System that continuously monitors voltage, current, and temperature — preventing overcharging and thermal issues while enhancing performance and longevity.

High-tech controller

A sophisticated electronic device overseeing complex systems through advanced algorithms, sensors, and real-time data monitoring — ensuring optimal performance, safety, and adaptability across electric and smart systems.

Advanced Tec charger

An advanced charger that recharges batteries efficiently and safely through adaptive algorithms, temperature regulation, and real-time monitoring — extending battery lifespan and reducing the risk of overcharging.

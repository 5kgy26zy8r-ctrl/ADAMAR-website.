How it works

Every Adamar draws from the same advanced lithium-ion battery platform, offered in greater-capacity options for riders who need longer distances. The pack is designed with an automatic eject for charging, so getting from empty to full is as simple as it should be — no matter which of the seven models it's powering.

Because the battery platform is shared, improvements made for one model — better cell chemistry, faster charging, longer cycle life — benefit every rider across the range, not just one bike.
